A browser with Wasm GC and tail call support is required for this demo. We recommend using either Mozilla Firefox or Google Chrome.

Baba Is You

Put a level, the words, grammar, physics and projector pages and the wasd controls on the table. Then point the keyboard at the level using its whisker (the whiskers page hands them out) and find a way to win.

Move with w/a/s/d, undo with u and reset with r. Once a level is solved it stops listening to keys; lifting it off the table resets it. Avoid q and e: they rotate the page under the mouse.

This started as a copy of the Sokoban pages, with the rules page swapped out. In Baba Is You the rules of the game are objects on the board: text tiles that form statements like BABA IS YOU or WALL IS STOP. Push the text around and the rules change.

The words page claims what each word is: BABA is a noun, IS is a verb, YOU is a property. The grammar page has one generic rule: every verb claims the two rows of three it sits in the middle of, horizontally and vertically. A second rule checks whether such a row reads noun-verb-property, and if so claims it as a rule of the level, like (baba is you). The physics page decides what those rules mean: YOU moves, STOP blocks, PUSH gets pushed, and YOU touching WIN wins.

Moving needs every active rule at once, but rules are derived one by one during a tick. So a level collects the rules claimed about it during one tick and claims them as a single list the next, as long as nothing changed in between. Moves wait until the rules have settled.
