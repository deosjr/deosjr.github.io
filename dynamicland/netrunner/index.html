Playing

Everything you do is putting a page down somewhere. Put a wand or the installer down with its whisker on a projected button to press it; pick it up and put it down again to press it again. The Corp's installer and wand point down at the board, the Runner's wand and run token point up.

  • Gain 1¢ / Draw 1: buttons under each identity, on that player's turn.
  • Flip a card: hover over it and press f. A face-down card can't be read, so the table doesn't see it.
  • Install (Corp): put the installer on "+ ice" in front of a server, or on "+ new remote". The table adds a slot; lay the card in it face down.
  • Rez: flip the card face up in its slot. Assets any time, ice only while the Runner approaches it.
  • Advance: put the Corp wand on a remote's slot, then on "Advance".
  • Score, play an Operation or Event: put the card in the resolution box. If more than one slot could hold the agenda, point the Corp wand at the right one.
  • Remove a slot: put the installer on it, then the Corp wand on "Remove".
  • Install (Runner): put a program or resource in the rig.
  • Run: put the run token below a server with its whisker pointing at it. Lift it and put it down again for the next run.
  • During a run: the Corp rezzes or presses Pass; the Runner breaks subroutines, lets the rest fire, continues or jacks out, and lays the accessed card face up in the resolution box (put it back face down afterwards).

In this version: one card accessed per run, nothing to access in Archives, agenda text ignored, no traces, tags, damage or viruses. Deck counts are nominal 30-card decks; all the cards are on the shelves, so draw honestly. Avoid q and e unless you mean to: they rotate the page under the mouse.
