Reus

A first version of Reus 2 on a projected table, for several players at once. Put the planet in the middle of the table, then the codex, the whiskers and the projector. Every player takes a giant: its page is also its pointer, so whatever its whisker points at, the giant acts on.

  • Walk: point the whisker at the planet's ring. The giant walks around the planet to that spot.
  • Plant: point the whisker just past the ring, along your giant's own direction, where its two faint dots are. Its biotica appear; hold the whisker on one for a second to plant it in the patch the giant stands on. Planting over a bioticum replaces it.
  • Wait: point anywhere else and the giant stays where it is.

Turn a page with q and e to aim its whisker. Each patch shows what grows there and its yield in food · gold · science; the planet's total is in the middle. Biotica help their neighbours: Blueberry gives more food next to a mineral, Moss Agate more gold next to a plant, and a Stoat more gold next to another critter, such as a Rabbit.

Lifting a giant's page takes the giant off the planet, and putting it back returns it where it stood. Lifting the planet starts the world over. When two giants plant in the same patch at the same moment, the planet lets the first giant on the shelf go first.
